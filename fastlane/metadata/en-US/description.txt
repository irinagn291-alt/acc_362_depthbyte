Join Nori on a deep-sea expedition through C17 programming. Explore 100 sequential dives across 10 sectors, descending 30 meters with each completed dive.

Start with output and numeric operations, then progress through control flow, arrays, functions, pointers, records, memory management, debugging, and algorithms.

Each dive includes a briefing, a C listing, and two multiple-choice checks. Predict the console output and choose a missing code fragment. If an answer is incorrect, Nori offers a repair note so you can adjust it and try again. Switch between the lesson excerpt and the complete program as you study.

Track your progress on the Bridge, browse unlocked dives in the Depth Chart, and revisit completed dives. Recover a hardware part after each sector and work toward clean dives by answering both checks correctly on the first try.

The curriculum and artwork are bundled with the app. Progress is stored on your device. The interface is in English.
